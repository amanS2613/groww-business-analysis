# Groww Business Analyst / Senior Business Analyst Hiring Assignment

**Objective**: This assignment tests data handling, analytical problem solving, business judgement and the ability to turn data into clear, actionable recommendations.

**Data**: Two sets of data are attached: user data (`users.csv`) and order data split by product (`stocks_orders.csv`, `fno_orders.csv`, `mf_orders.csv`, `ipo_orders.csv`, `commodity_orders.csv`). All files can be joined on the user id (`third_party_id`). Column definitions are in `data_dictionary.md`. Signups cover April to September 2026 and order data ends on 31 Oct 2026.

**Output**: Any format is fine (ppt, doc, html, etc.), and creativity is appreciated. Every answer should be self-explanatory, with the approach clearly explained. Maximum length is 10 pages(total).

**Use of AI tools**: AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, etc.) are allowed. If you use them, you must submit your full prompt history (chat export or prompt file) along with your analysis. Your prompt history will also be used in the evaluation.

---

**Question 1 — Who is worth focusing on?** The team can only focus its retention efforts on a small slice of users. Make the case for which slice.
Define what "high value" means for Groww — there is no single right answer, but
wrong answers exist — then show, with evidence, how high-value users differ from
the rest. End with the 2–3 differences strong enough to act on.

**Question 2 — The watchlist.** Every week, the team can give personal attention to about 20
newly signed-up users. They only have each user's first 7 days after signup to choose them, and a
user's value can change over time. Which 20 users should they pick each week so that the most of
them turn out to be high value (as defined in Q1) by day 90 after signup?

**Question 3 — Where should the next marketing rupee go?** Marketing wants to move budget towards
the acquisition channels that bring the most valuable users. Which
channels should get more budget, and which should get less?

**Question 4 — Revenue concentration and the next bet.** How concentrated is Groww's revenue in
certain pockets, and what should be the next bet if that source
shrank significantly?
